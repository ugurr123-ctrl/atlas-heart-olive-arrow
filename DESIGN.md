# Veira mobile design

Preserve the established Turkish interface: midnight #15182c surfaces, gold #e8ca79 actions, ivory #f7f1df headings and muted #c4c5d8 text. Georgia/serif display headings complement the existing sans-serif controls. Collage photos, torn paper and washi remain the signature artwork.

Premium uses a compact native dialog with two equally sized monthly/yearly options, clear localized recurring prices, a restore action and real policy links. No rotating headings or added ambient effects. Existing shared toast feedback is reused. Store-derived prices override Turkish reference prices. The purchase button stays unavailable until the selected store product loads.

PNG export belongs to the board export service: native file/share sheet on phones, a PNG download in browsers. Cancellation produces no false success. Never grant Premium from localStorage.
